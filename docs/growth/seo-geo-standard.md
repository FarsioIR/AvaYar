# AvaYar SEO + GEO standard

AvaYar must optimize for both traditional search discovery and generative-engine discovery. SEO and GEO are complementary requirements; neither replaces the other.

## SEO requirements

Every public indexable surface should support:

- crawlable semantic HTML and stable canonical URLs;
- unique title and meta description aligned with search intent;
- correct language and locale metadata;
- structured heading hierarchy;
- internal linking between product, feature, use-case, help, and comparison pages;
- sitemap and robots directives appropriate to the environment;
- structured data where it truthfully represents the page content;
- strong Core Web Vitals and mobile usability;
- useful, original Persian content rather than thin keyword pages.

## GEO requirements

Public content should also be easy for generative systems to understand, retrieve, attribute, and summarize accurately:

- define the AvaYar entity consistently: product name, purpose, capabilities, ownership/ecosystem relationship, and supported languages;
- provide concise factual answer blocks for high-intent questions;
- publish deep FAQs with direct answers and unambiguous terminology;
- use structured feature, use-case, comparison, limitation, privacy, and troubleshooting content;
- keep factual claims attributable and update dates visible where freshness matters;
- avoid vague marketing language when a concrete product fact can be stated instead;
- keep terminology consistent across pages so entity resolution is reliable;
- expose useful structured metadata while ensuring the visible page contains the same factual substance.

## Content architecture

AvaYar's public information architecture should eventually include indexable pages for:

1. Product overview and core value proposition.
2. Persian text-to-speech / read-aloud capability.
3. Persian and English summarization and translation use cases where supported.
4. Browser-extension workflows.
5. Mobile workflows when released.
6. Feature-specific help and troubleshooting.
7. Comparisons based on verifiable product capabilities.
8. Privacy, security, limitations, and provider dependencies.
9. Frequently asked questions and glossary/entity definitions.

## Quality rules

- Do not publish automatically generated pages merely to increase page count.
- Do not make unsupported superiority, accuracy, or availability claims.
- Do not state that VPN guarantees Gemini access; network routing is only one prerequisite and Google-side access policy may still reject requests.
- Persian pages should use natural Persian terminology and presentation; English pages should remain fully English.
- SEO/GEO work must preserve accessibility, performance, and user experience.

## Measurement

SEO and GEO should be evaluated separately but managed together. Track traditional search impressions/clicks/indexation alongside generative-engine referrals, brand/entity mentions, citation visibility where observable, and conversion from informational entry pages into product use.

This standard is an architectural requirement for future public AvaYar surfaces and content work.
